Frequently Asked Questions

Where do samples come from?

You can record from the microphone, import any audio file from your device, record audio playing elsewhere on your device (system audio, via a Broadcast Extension), or pick one of the 12 samples built into the app's library.

Why does Wax Deck ask for microphone access?

Only to let you record a sample directly from the mic. If you never use that feature, the mic is never active. Recordings stay on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

What does "system audio" recording do?

It uses iOS's Broadcast Extension to capture audio from other apps or the device itself while you record, so you can turn any sound into a scratchable sample. The captured audio is written to a private, on-device App Group container that only Wax Deck reads — it is never sent anywhere else.

What does the "Sync" feature do?

Sync uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity to let nearby devices on the same Wax Deck session follow one device's deck in real time (e.g. for a two-performer act). It only talks to other nearby devices over local Wi-Fi/Bluetooth — never to the internet, and never automatically; it only activates when you explicitly start a session.

Can I use Wax Deck inside GarageBand or another host app?

Yes — Wax Deck includes two AUv3 Audio Unit plug-ins: a scratchable instrument, and a capture effect you insert on any track to grab whatever's playing and scratch it live.

Privacy Policy

Wax Deck is built to keep your audio and performances on your device. Specifically:

  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No account or sign-in — the app does not know who you are.
  • No data is ever sent to any server operated by the developer or anyone else.
  • Microphone is used only when you choose to record a sample from it, and only while you are actively recording.
  • System audio recording (Broadcast Extension) captures audio only while you have a broadcast actively running, writes it to a private on-device App Group container, and never transmits it anywhere.
  • Sync (MultipeerConnectivity) communicates only with other nearby devices over local Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, only while you have started a sync session — it never reaches the internet.
  • Samples, chop/loop settings, lesson progress, and recorded performances are stored only on your device.
  • Exported audio or video leaves the device only when you explicitly share it via the system share sheet.
